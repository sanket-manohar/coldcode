## 🎬 ABOUT THE SERIES

**COLD CODE** is an entertainment series on YouTube about **real crime stories that were solved by technology**, with **English**, **हिन्दी** and **Español** audio.

Each episode tells one true story that stayed unsolved for years, and the invention or technology that finally cracked it.

### 🎯 Our purpose

| 👁️ Stay vigilant | 🛡️ Protect people | 🚫 Stop crime |
|---|---|---|
| Notice the crimes happening around us, online and offline, before they reach you or your family. | Keep your family, friends and community safe. | Technology exposes the truth, so crime has nowhere to hide. |

> **These stories have one purpose: to keep us vigilant against the crimes happening around us, to protect people and to help stop crime. Never to misuse technology.**
